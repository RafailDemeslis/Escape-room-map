Escape Room FAQ

How do I find escape rooms in a specific city?

Open the Map page, then search by room name or company and use location radius to focus on one city.

How do I find horror escape rooms?

In the Map filters, select Horror, Psychological horror, Spooky, or Splatter categories.

How do I find puzzle or riddle escape rooms?

Select the Riddles category to focus on puzzle-heavy and clue-based rooms.

Can I submit an escape room?

Yes. On the Map page, open the Submit tab and send the room details for review.
